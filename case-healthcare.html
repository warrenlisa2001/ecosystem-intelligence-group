Use Case Narrative

Reducing Patient Wait Times at Tertiary Care Facility

Healthcare organisations are under pressure to improve throughput, manage rising costs, and deploy AI responsibly while still protecting quality of care. In this environment, operational inefficiency, procurement leakage, clinical data silos, and patient flow bottlenecks compound one another quickly.

In healthcare, better flow is not just a scheduling improvement — it is a system-level gain across cost, experience, and clinical delivery.

Challenge

Healthcare organisations are under pressure to improve throughput, manage rising costs, and deploy AI responsibly while still protecting quality of care. In this environment, operational inefficiency, procurement leakage, clinical data silos, and patient flow bottlenecks compound one another quickly.

  • Operational inefficiency across departments, scheduling, and resource allocation
  • Rising procurement costs without clear line of sight into usage and demand
  • Clinical data silos that prevent a complete operational and care-performance view
  • Patient flow bottlenecks that increase waits, reduce satisfaction, and strain staff capacity

What EIG assessed

  • How patient demand, scheduling, staffing, and bed/resource use interacted across the care journey
  • Where operational delays were being created by fragmented data or weak escalation logic
  • How procurement and supply patterns affected daily delivery performance
  • Which AI use cases could create fast wins without adding governance risk

Key Findings

Flow problems were systemic, not isolated

Wait times were being driven by handoff friction across multiple departments rather than one single bottleneck.

Cost pressure and operational pressure were linked

Poor visibility into demand and utilisation created unnecessary spend and uneven resource deployment.

Data existed but was not connected

Clinical, operational, and supply information was present, but not assembled into a usable command layer.

AI value depended on workflow integration

Analytics needed to sit inside operational decisions — not outside them — to improve care delivery.

Solution Model

EIG's solution model combined patient-flow intelligence, operational forecasting, supply optimisation, and clinical decision support to create a more connected healthcare operating rhythm.

AI-driven predictive analytics

  • Forecast demand patterns, likely congestion points, and resource stress before service levels deteriorate
  • Support better daily planning and exception handling across clinical operations

Intelligent supply chain optimization

  • Connect procurement and consumption data to reduce excess cost and improve availability
  • Give operations teams clearer visibility into what is driving usage, replenishment, and variance

Clinical decision support systems

  • Bring timely intelligence closer to frontline decisions
  • Improve coordination between clinical quality priorities and operational performance management

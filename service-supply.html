Supply Chain & Procurement

Beyond Cost Cutting.
Building Resilience.

The best supply chains are not just efficient — they are resilient, intelligent and strategically aligned. The pandemic and geopolitical disruption exposed the cost of building supply chains purely for efficiency. EIG helps organisations build supply chains that are both.

We bring deep procurement expertise, supply chain intelligence and ecosystem-level thinking to every engagement — identifying savings, strengthening supplier relationships and building the operational resilience that creates sustainable competitive advantage.

Supply Chain Diagnostics
Comprehensive mapping and analysis of your supply chain — identifying vulnerabilities, inefficiencies and opportunities across the entire network.
Procurement Optimisation
Reviewing your procurement strategy, processes and supplier relationships to identify and capture measurable value across all spend categories.
Resilience by Design
Building supply chains that withstand disruption — with diversification, real-time visibility and contingency built in from the ground up.

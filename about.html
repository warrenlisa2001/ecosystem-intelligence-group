About EIG

Understanding complexity. Creating measurable value.

Ecosystem Intelligence Group is an Strategic Intelligence & Transformation Practice of SIB Consulting, bringing together expertise in operational performance, sustainability, intelligence and growth.

Part of SIB Consulting — a multidisciplinary firm connecting intelligence to execution across the GCC and beyond.

What We Are

Built on one powerful observation.
Most firms solve problems. We understand systems.

Ecosystem Intelligence Group was built on a simple but powerful observation: most advisory firms help organisations solve individual problems. We help them understand the entire system.

We work with organisations navigating genuine complexity — where decisions in one part of the business ripple unpredictably through supply chains, sustainability commitments, workforce capability and economic performance.

Combining more than 100 years of combined leadership experience across economic development, sustainability economics, supply chain, architecture, interior design, education, healthcare, IT, AI and strategic planning, EIG delivers measurable impact where it matters most.

Our team has worked across government, healthcare, manufacturing, logistics, education, energy, economic development, architecture and information technology — bringing practical expertise, not theoretical frameworks, to every engagement across the GCC and internationally.

Where Most Are Today

Compliance-Driven Data Collection

Data collected to satisfy regulatory requirements and reporting obligations. Sits in silos — answers the questions of yesterday. Tells you what happened, not what to do next. Treated as a cost and reporting burden, not a strategic asset. Leadership tolerates it rather than relies on it.

Where EIG Takes You

Ecosystem Intelligence. Strategic Advantage.

Data becomes the foundation of strategic decision-making. Connected across operations, supply chains, sustainability and financial performance. Creates a living picture of how the organisation creates value. Every decision faster, clearer and more confident. Leadership relies on it — competitive advantage, not compliance cost.

Digital Transformation: Beyond the Buzzword

Why most digital transformations fail — and how to build ones that succeed.

Intelligence Brief — February 2026

Digital. When Strategy Meets Reality.

Seventy percent of digital transformations fail to deliver their intended value. Not because the technology is inadequate, but because the transformation is treated as a technology project rather than a business transformation.

Why Transformations Fail

Technology-first thinking. Change without capability. Fragmented ownership. Activity metrics. Abandoned at go-live. Five common patterns of failed digital transformations.

The EIG Approach to Digital Transformation

EIG helps organisations design digital transformations that deliver measurable value: outcome definition before technology selection, operating model design, capability building, governance and measurement, and execution discipline that stays until delivery is real.

"Digital transformation is not about technology. It is about business performance. The technology is the enabler. The transformation is the outcome."

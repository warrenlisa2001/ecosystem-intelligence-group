AI Sales Order Validation
Live
⬡

Enter an order and submit.
Three AI agents will validate it in real time.

1
Stock availability — Agent queries DC inventory. If order qty > 85% of available stock → partial risk flag. If > 100% → hold.
Stock Risk = Order Qty ÷ DC Stock
›0.85 warn | ›1.0 hold
2
MOQ validation — Channel-specific minimum: Modern Trade 100 cases, HOD 20, Retail 30. Orders below MOQ auto-flagged.
IF Order Qty < Customer MOQ → REJECT
3
Delivery date eligibility — Cross-references EDD against customer delivery calendar. Fri/Sat = non-delivery for most Modern Trade. Agent suggests next valid date.
EDD valid = date ∈ customer delivery calendar
Fri(5) / Sat(6) → flag + next valid suggested
4
Duplicate detection — Scans open orders for same Customer + SKU + EDD within T-24hrs. Root cause of 11,244 wrong-booking cancellations (82% of Masafi's total).
Duplicate = (Customer + SKU + EDD) matches open order within T-24hrs
5
Risk scoring — Four dimensions combined into a 0–100 risk score.
Risk = (Stock×0.4) + (MOQ×0.3) + (EDD×0.2) + (Dup×0.1)
›60 Hold | 30–60 Warn | ‹30 Approve
6
Prioritisation decision — Final routing: Approve → operations. Hold → supervisor. Reject → preseller notified with reason code. Every decision logged with timestamp, order value, and structured reason code — replacing the 854 free-text codes in Masafi's current system.
Agent 02
AI Route & Load Optimisation
Live
⬡

Select region and route.
Agent reads 90 days of sell-through per customer stop.

1
Historical sell-through baseline — 90 days of route data per stop. Calculates mean daily units sold + standard deviation. Al Ain σ=0.55 on mean near plan — high variance the agent dampens.
Baseline(stop) = Mean(sold, T-90) × DayCoeff
Sun=1.15 | Mon=0.95 | Tue–Wed=1.0 | Thu=1.10 | Sat=0.85
2
Demand signal adjustment — Baseline weighted for: recent 14-day trend (1.4× weight), active promotions (uplift flag), seasonal index (UAE Q1 water 1.5L peaks ~18%).
Adjusted = Baseline × TrendFactor × PromoMultiplier × SeasonIndex
3
Safety stock buffer — Configurable buffer above adjusted demand. Default 8%. Reduced to 5% for low-variance stops (σ<0.2). Increased to 12% for high-variance (σ>0.4).
Recommended(stop) = Adjusted × (1 + Buffer%)
4
Vehicle capacity constraint — Sum of all stop recommendations checked against truck capacity (6-tonne ≈ 320 cases 500ml equivalent). If total >90% capacity, allocations re-weighted from highest to lowest variance.
Σ Recommended(stops) ≤ Vehicle Capacity × 0.90
5
AED saving calculation — Reduction in loaded cases × average price per case (AED 95) × 313 working days for annualised saving. Al Ain target: from 35.7% returns to <5%.
Saving = (Current Load − AI Load) × AED 95 per case
Annual = Daily saving × 313 working days
6
Confidence scoring — High = 60+ records, low variance. Medium = 30–60 records or moderate variance. Low = <30 records → flagged for human review before acting on recommendation.

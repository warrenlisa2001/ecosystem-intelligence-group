AI Route & Load Optimisation
Live
Van Sales is separate from the e-commerce channel. The driver loads the vehicle at the depot before dawn using habit or supervisor instruction — no structured demand data exists. Agent 02 reads 90 days of actual sell-through per customer stop and gives the driver the right load before they leave.
⬡

Select a region and route.
Agent reads 90 days of sell-through per stop.

1
Historical sell-through baseline (T-90) — Reads last 90 route records per customer stop. Calculates mean daily units sold and standard deviation. Al Ain routes show σ=0.55 on a mean near plan — the agent dampens this variance.
Baseline(stop) = Mean(sold, T-90) × DayCoeff
Sun=1.15 | Mon=0.95 | Tue–Wed=1.0 | Thu=1.10 | Sat=0.85
2
Recent trend weighting — Last 14 days weighted at 1.4× vs T-90 baseline to capture demand shifts more quickly than a flat 90-day mean. Active promotions trigger an uplift flag. UAE seasonal index applied (Q1 water 1.5L peaks ~18%).
Adjusted = Baseline × TrendFactor(T-14) × PromoMultiplier × SeasonIndex
3
Safety buffer by variance band — Buffer added above adjusted demand: 5% for low-variance stops (σ<0.2), 8% default, 12% for high-variance stops (σ>0.4). Prevents stockouts at high-demand stops without over-loading the vehicle.
Recommended(stop) = Adjusted × (1 + Buffer%)
4
Vehicle capacity constraint — Sum of all stop recommendations checked against truck capacity (6-tonne ≈ 320 cases 500ml equivalent). If total exceeds 90% of capacity, allocations re-weighted proportionally from highest to lowest variance stop.
Σ Recommended(stops) ≤ Vehicle Capacity × 0.90
5
AED saving calculation — Reduction in loaded cases × AED 95 average per case × 313 working days for annualised saving. Al Ain target: from 35.7% returns to under 5%.
Daily saving = (Current Load − AI Load) × AED 95
Annual = Daily saving × 313 working days
6
Confidence scoring — High = 60+ records, low variance. Medium = 30–60 records. Low = under 30 records → flagged for supervisor review before acting on recommendation. Confidence displayed per stop in the output table.

Intelligence & Data Validation — 4 to 6 Weeks
Recommended Phase 0
What we say to the Chairman

"Chairman, our prototype demonstrates what is possible using the data currently available. The next phase is not AI implementation. The next phase is establishing confidence in the information that will ultimately drive decisions, automation and future agentic capabilities."

Before any large-scale AI deployment, Masafi needs a structured validation phase. Not to slow the programme — to ensure every decision, every automation, and every AED invested is built on trusted information. We are not overselling. We are saying: we built something valuable, we found opportunities, we found constraints, now we validate the foundations.

Week 1
Data Discovery
What exists and who owns it?
  • ›What systems exist — Oracle, Mobiato, Locus, Excel?
  • ›What reports exist and who produces them?
  • ›What data has been provided vs what data exists?
  • ›Who owns each data source — name and role?
Week 2
Data Validation
Is the data clean enough to trust?
  • ›Missing data — what gaps exist across systems?
  • ›Duplicates — orders, accounts, SKUs, reason codes
  • ›Inconsistencies — do systems agree with each other?
  • ›Process gaps — where does data fall off the record?
Week 3
Business Validation
"Does this reflect reality?"
  • ›Sit with Sales — do the cancellation numbers match their experience?
  • ›Sit with Supply Chain — does the inventory data reflect what they see?
  • ›Sit with Warehouse — does fill attainment match Ashwin's numbers?
  • ›Sit with Finance — do the AED figures tie to their records?
Often the answer is: "No." That is not failure. That is the most valuable finding.
Week 4
Intelligence Validation
Can we trust the AI's inputs?
  • ›Revenue leakage assumptions — is AED 23.7M the right number?
  • ›Inventory assumptions — are DC stock figures reliable?
  • ›Forecasting assumptions — is the AOP a valid baseline?
  • ›Service level assumptions — does 60–70% fill reflect reality?
Weeks 5–6 (Optional) — Confidence Scoring
✓ High Confidence
Validated by data AND by the business teams in Week 3. These findings can drive AI agent decisions and automation from Day 1 of deployment.
~ Medium Confidence
Supported by evidence but requires further validation with specific teams or systems. AI agents flag these decisions for human review rather than automating them.
? Hypothesis
Requires further investigation before acting. These are tracked as open questions — not suppressed — and resolved before any automation is built on them.
If the Chairman asks: "How quickly can we implement this?"

"We can demonstrate value immediately. We would recommend a 4–6 week Intelligence & Data Validation phase before any large-scale AI deployment. That gives Masafi confidence that future decisions, automation and investment are built on trusted information."

SKU eligibility & active status — Agent checks the ordered SKU is active, not discontinued, and eligible for this customer's account type. Inactive SKU auto-validation failures are one of Masafi's four stated causes of missed orders in the process flow.
IF SKU.status ≠ 'active' OR SKU ∉ customer.eligible_skus → REJECT
2
Stock availability at DC — Queries live DC inventory. If requested qty > 85% of available stock → partial fulfilment risk. If > 100% → hold and trigger auto stock transfer request from factory DC (as per Masafi's documented process).
Stock ratio = Order Qty ÷ DC Available
›0.85 → warn | ›1.0 → hold + auto STO trigger to Factory DC
3
Delivery slot validation — Checks EDD against customer's delivery calendar. E-commerce orders via app auto-set EDD to next working day. Agent validates the slot is open, the DC cutoff (8pm) has not passed, and the customer's location is on the scheduled route for that day.
EDD valid = slot.status = 'open' AND order_time < DC_cutoff(20:00)
Invalid slot → agent suggests next available EDD
4
Pricing & eligibility check — Validates pricing matches customer's contracted rate and that the order meets minimum value or quantity thresholds for the channel. Pricing mismatches are a documented cause of missed orders in Mobiato.
IF order.price ≠ customer.contracted_price → flag for CS review
5
Duplicate detection — Scans open orders for same account + SKU + EDD placed within T-24hrs. Root cause of 11,244 of Masafi's 13,671 cancellations. A structured reason code is logged (replacing the 854 free-text variants currently in the system).
Duplicate = (Account + SKU + EDD) ∈ open_orders within T-24hrs
6
Risk score & routing decision — Combined risk score 0–100. Approved orders push automatically to Mobiato for DC processing. Held orders alert the CS team with a structured reason code. Every decision is logged with timestamp and order value — creating the clean audit trail Masafi currently lacks.
Risk = (Stock×0.4)+(EDD×0.25)+(Dup×0.2)+(Price×0.15)
‹30 → Approve to Mobiato | 30–60 → CS review | ›60 → Hold

Van Sales is separate from the e-commerce channel. The driver loads the vehicle at the depot before dawn using habit or supervisor instruction — no structured demand data exists. Agent 02 reads 90 days of actual sell-through per customer stop and gives the driver the right load before they leave.

Select a region and route.
Agent reads 90 days of sell-through per stop.

1
Historical sell-through baseline (T-90) — Reads last 90 route records per customer stop. Calculates mean daily units sold and standard deviation. Al Ain routes show σ=0.55 on a mean near plan — the agent dampens this variance.
Baseline(stop) = Mean(sold, T-90) × DayCoeff
Sun=1.15 | Mon=0.95 | Tue–Wed=1.0 | Thu=1.10 | Sat=0.85
2
Recent trend weighting — Last 14 days weighted at 1.4× vs T-90 baseline to capture demand shifts more quickly than a flat 90-day mean. Active promotions trigger an uplift flag. UAE seasonal index applied (Q1 water 1.5L peaks ~18%).
Adjusted = Baseline × TrendFactor(T-14) × PromoMultiplier × SeasonIndex
3
Safety buffer by variance band — Buffer added above adjusted demand: 5% for low-variance stops (σ<0.2), 8% default, 12% for high-variance stops (σ>0.4). Prevents stockouts at high-demand stops without over-loading the vehicle.
Recommended(stop) = Adjusted × (1 + Buffer%)
4
Vehicle capacity constraint — Sum of all stop recommendations checked against truck capacity (6-tonne ≈ 320 cases 500ml equivalent). If total exceeds 90% of capacity, allocations re-weighted proportionally from highest to lowest variance stop.
Σ Recommended(stops) ≤ Vehicle Capacity × 0.90
5
AED saving calculation — Reduction in loaded cases × AED 95 average per case × 313 working days for annualised saving. Al Ain target: from 35.7% returns to under 5%.
Daily saving = (Current Load − AI Load) × AED 95
Annual = Daily saving × 313 working days
6
Confidence scoring — High = 60+ records, low variance. Medium = 30–60 records. Low = under 30 records → flagged for supervisor review before acting on recommendation. Confidence displayed per stop in the output table.

Clause 4.2 · Discovery Phase · 60 Days

Scope of Work — The Discovery Phase.

Under the Consultancy Service Agreement, WP1 comprises four defined outputs delivered over sixty days. WP2 and WP3 remain out of scope until WP1 findings are reviewed.

a
Operational stocktake and integrated supply-chain baseline
›
An end-to-end factual view of how Masafi operates today.
b
Gap analysis across the five active planning systems
›
An integration-ready view of the current data and system estate.
c
Agentic AI evaluation matrix
›
A prioritised framework of where agentic AI is applied first.
d
Live Masafi-OS warehouse capacity-planning demonstrator
›
A working demonstrator built on Masafi's own data, tangible evidence of agentic AI in practice.

How WP1 Is Governed

Protected on both sides.

Mutual and ongoing. Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it solely for the Programme — an obligation that survives termination of the Agreement.

Intellectual Property
›
Clause 17
The Deliverables and Masafi's own operational data belong to Masafi outright. SIB retains Background IP — its AI models, frameworks and methodologies — licensed to Masafi solely as embedded in the Deliverables.

›
Clause 8
Either party may exit on 30 working days' written notice. Work in progress continues to be paid, and accrued rights — including SIB's right to fees already earned — are unaffected.

Government & Public Sector

Creating Ecosystem Intelligence Across a Regional Economy

A regional economic authority was responsible for supporting more than 40,000 businesses across multiple sectors including manufacturing, logistics, technology, healthcare, education and professional services.

While significant volumes of data existed across licensing, investment, workforce development, infrastructure and economic development programmes, the information remained fragmented across departments, agencies and reporting systems.

Leadership lacked a single view of ecosystem performance. Critical questions could not be answered quickly: which sectors were creating the most economic value, where businesses were facing growth barriers, which initiatives were generating measurable outcomes, where investment opportunities were emerging, and which risks required intervention before impacting economic performance.

“Data existed everywhere. Intelligence existed nowhere.”

Challenge

Reporting was largely retrospective, making it difficult to identify trends, opportunities and risks in real time. Leadership needed a way to connect fragmented information into a single decision-support environment that reflected the true dynamics of the wider economy.

EIG Assessment

EIG conducted a comprehensive Ecosystem Intelligence Assessment to understand how information, investment, businesses, infrastructure, workforce capability and policy interacted across the wider economic ecosystem.

  • Ecosystem mapping
  • Stakeholder analysis
  • Economic intelligence diagnostics
  • Data maturity assessment
  • Value leakage analysis
  • AI readiness assessment
  • Leadership decision-making review
  • Reporting and governance assessment

Key Findings

Fragmented Intelligence

Information was spread across multiple departments, creating inconsistent reporting and limiting leadership visibility.

Delayed Decision-Making

Reporting cycles focused on historical performance rather than emerging opportunities and risks.

Limited Ecosystem Visibility

There was no single view connecting investment, business performance, workforce capability and infrastructure development.

Hidden Value Leakages

Growth, efficiency and optimisation opportunities were difficult to identify due to disconnected data sources.

AI Readiness Gaps

Opportunities existed to leverage advanced analytics, automation and agentic AI, but foundational intelligence architecture was required first.

EIG Solution

EIG designed an Ecosystem Intelligence Framework that connected multiple sources of information into a single decision-support environment.

  • Executive intelligence dashboards
  • Real-time ecosystem monitoring
  • Sector performance tracking
  • Economic value creation measurement
  • Investment impact analysis
  • Risk and opportunity indicators
  • AI readiness roadmap
  • Governance and intelligence framework

Outcomes

Real-Time Visibility

Leadership gained access to live ecosystem intelligence dashboards across key sectors, programmes and performance indicators.

Faster Strategic Decisions

Decision-making cycles were reduced through automated reporting and intelligence-driven insight.

Improved Investment Prioritisation

Investment decisions became more evidence-based through better visibility of impact and value creation opportunities.

Enhanced Performance Monitoring

Leadership could track performance, identify emerging risks and respond proactively to changing conditions.

Foundation for Agentic AI

The intelligence architecture established the foundations for future AI-enabled decision support and ecosystem management.

Strategic Impact

The organisation moved beyond traditional reporting and developed the capability to actively manage its economic ecosystem through connected intelligence. Rather than viewing sectors, programmes and stakeholders individually, leadership gained visibility into the relationships, dependencies and value flows that drive long-term economic performance.

“The most competitive economies are not built on data alone. They are built on the ability to transform data into intelligence, intelligence into decisions and decisions into measurable value.”
